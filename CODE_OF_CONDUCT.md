# Code of conduct

We want a technical community that is welcoming, patient, and safe.

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Discuss ideas and observable behavior, not personal characteristics.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, camera images, or device
  identifiers.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, and deliberate disruption are not
  accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the project and its users. Report sensitive conduct issues
privately to the repository owner.
